# Product

## Users
TUP-V Students (SIT trainees), Industry Partners (Employers), and University Coordinators. They use the platform to manage mandatory industrial training, certify hours, and facilitate communication between the university and industry.

## Product Purpose
A mission-critical enterprise platform that digitalizes the Supervised Industrial Training (SIT) lifecycle. It replaces paper-based workflows with a secure, real-time tracking system for hours, documents, and evaluations.

## Brand Personality
Institutional, Professional, Authoritative.
The tone is serious and archival, evoking trust, rigor, and official status.

## Anti-references
Generic "SaaS-y" landing pages with soft, oversized corners, pastel palettes, playful illustrations, or vibrant neon gradients. The system must never feel like a commercial startup; it must feel like a university institution.

## Design Principles
- **Archival Integrity**: The interface should feel like an extension of the university's official records—authoritative and permanent.
- **Structural Precision**: Use high-contrast grids and technical typography to emphasize clarity and accuracy.
- **Refined Interactivity**: While maintaining institutional sobriety, use custom deep-crimson gradients and sophisticated micro-animations to guide user attention and signal interactive "floating islands."
- **Functional Density**: Prioritize information density and clear typographic hierarchy over decorative whitespace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. High contrast is essential for legibility across different devices and lighting conditions.
