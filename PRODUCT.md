# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
TUP-V Students (SIT trainees), Industry Partners (Employers), and University Coordinators. They use the platform to manage mandatory industrial training, certify hours, and facilitate communication between the university and industry.

## Product Purpose
A mission-critical enterprise platform that digitalizes the Supervised Industrial Training (SIT) lifecycle. It replaces paper-based workflows with a secure, real-time tracking system for hours, documents, and evaluations.

## Brand Personality
Institutional, Professional, Precise.
The tone is serious and academic, evoking trust, rigor, and official status.

## Anti-references
Generic "SaaS-y" landing pages with soft rounded corners, glassmorphism as a default, playful illustrations, or vibrant gradient backgrounds that feel more like a startup than a university institution.

## Design Principles
- **Institutional Authority**: The interface should feel like an extension of the university's official records.
- **Structural Precision**: Use high-contrast grids and technical typography to emphasize clarity and accuracy.
- **Functional Sobriety**: Every visual element must earn its place; prioritize information density over decorative whitespace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. High contrast is essential for legibility across different devices and lighting conditions.
