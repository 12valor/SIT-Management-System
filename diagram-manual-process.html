SIT Process Workflow · Legacy System

Manual SIT Inquiry & Logging

A visualization of the legacy, paper-based Supervised Industrial Training workflow, highlighting the physical handoffs, manual record-keeping, and verification delays that the digital system replaces.

1. Inquiry & Application

Step 1 to 4 · Phase Overview

  • Bulletin Board Posting: Students must physically visit the campus SIT office to view vacancy postings pinned to the board.
  • Paper Registration: Registration forms must be obtained and filled out by hand, risking transcription and legibility issues.
  • Eligibility Hurdles: Office staff must pull physical student folders to manually calculate cumulative GPAs and course requirements.

2. Placement & Delivery

Step 5 & 6 · Phase Overview

  • Physical Endorsement: Coordinators issue a printed endorsement letter with a wet-ink signature and university dry seal.
  • Hand-Delivery to Partners: Students travel directly to prospective company offices to hand over application packages.
  • Acceptance Documentation: Companies complete and sign a paper acceptance slip, which the student must physically return to campus.

3. Logging & Validation

Step 7 to 9 · Phase Overview

  • Bound Paper Logbooks: Trainees manually write detailed shift task descriptions and time entries inside a physical logbook daily.
  • Wet Signature Sign-offs: Company supervisors review and manually sign off on every shift or week, making it vulnerable to signatures being missed or forged.
  • Laborious Tallying: Coordinators manually review, verify signatures, and count hundreds of logbook hours, introducing potential calculation errors.
